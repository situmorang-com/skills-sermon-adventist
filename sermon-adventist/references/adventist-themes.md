# Adventist Doctrinal Themes — Quick Reference

Map of major Adventist distinctives to anchor texts and Ellen G. White works most associated with each. Use to identify whether a passage engages a distinctive, and where to look for support material.

Always verify EGW citations live via egwwritings.org before quoting.

---

## The Sabbath

**Anchor texts:** Genesis 2:1-3; Exodus 20:8-11; Deuteronomy 5:12-15; Isaiah 58:13-14; Mark 2:23-28; Luke 4:16; Hebrews 4:9-11; Revelation 14:6-12.

**EGW works:**
- *Patriarchs and Prophets* — chapters on Creation, Sinai
- *Desire of Ages* — Mark 2 / Luke 6 chapters on Sabbath conflicts
- *Great Controversy* — chapters on the change of the Sabbath, the seal of God

---

## The Sanctuary and Investigative Judgment

**Anchor texts:** Exodus 25–40; Leviticus 16; Daniel 7–9 (esp. 8:14, 9:24-27); Hebrews 8–10; Revelation 11:19; 14:6-7.

**EGW works:**
- *Patriarchs and Prophets* — chapters on the Sanctuary and its services
- *Great Controversy* — chapters on the Sanctuary, the Investigative Judgment
- *Christ in His Sanctuary* (compilation)

---

## The Second Coming

**Anchor texts:** John 14:1-3; Acts 1:9-11; 1 Thessalonians 4:13-18; 2 Thessalonians 1:7-10; Matthew 24; Revelation 1:7; 19:11-16; 22:7, 12, 20.

**EGW works:**
- *Great Controversy* — closing chapters
- *Desire of Ages* — passages on Christ's promises of return
- *Early Writings* — visions of the Second Coming

---

## State of the Dead / Conditional Immortality

**Anchor texts:** Genesis 2:7; 3:19; Ecclesiastes 9:5-6, 10; Psalm 6:5; 115:17; 146:4; Job 14:10-14; Daniel 12:2; John 11:11-14; 1 Thessalonians 4:13-18; 1 Corinthians 15:51-54; Revelation 20:5-6, 14.

**EGW works:**
- *Great Controversy* — chapter "Can Our Dead Speak to Us?"
- *Patriarchs and Prophets* — chapter on the temptation in Eden ("Ye shall not surely die")

---

## Three Angels' Messages

**Anchor text:** Revelation 14:6-12 (with broader Revelation context).

**EGW works:**
- *Great Controversy* — multiple chapters
- *Early Writings*

---

## Salvation by Grace through Faith

**Anchor texts:** Genesis 15:6; Habakkuk 2:4; John 3:16; Romans 3:21-26; 4; 5:1-11; 8; Galatians 2:16-21; 3:11; Ephesians 2:1-10; Titus 3:4-7.

**EGW works:**
- *Steps to Christ* — the foundational SDA discipleship classic
- *Christ's Object Lessons* — esp. chapters on the Pharisee and publican, the wedding garment
- *Selected Messages*, vol. 1 — sections on righteousness by faith
- *Faith and Works*
- 1888 Minneapolis material

## The Spirit of Prophecy

**Anchor texts:** Joel 2:28-29; Ephesians 4:11-14; Revelation 12:17; 19:10; 1 Corinthians 12.

**EGW works:**
- *Selected Messages*, vols. 1-3
- *Last Day Events* (compilation)
- *The Great Controversy*, introduction

**EGW works:**
- *Ministry of Healing*
- *Counsels on Diet and Foods*
- *Counsels on Health*

## The Great Controversy

**Anchor texts:** Isaiah 14:12-15; Ezekiel 28:11-17; Revelation 12; Job 1–2.

**EGW works:**
- *The Great Controversy* (entire)
- *Patriarchs and Prophets* — opening chapters
- *Desire of Ages* — the temptation chapters

## When Not to Force a Theme

If the passage is on grief, marriage, work, anxiety, generosity, or another general Christian topic, **don't shoehorn in a distinctive Adventist doctrine.** Let the text speak. Adventist preaching is faithful to *all* of Scripture, not just to the distinctives.

The distinctives shine brightest when they emerge naturally from texts that genuinely carry them.
