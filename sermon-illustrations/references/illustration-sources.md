# Illustration Sources by Theme

Themed pointers to wells of well-attested illustrations. Use these to find candidate illustrations, then verify before using. Quotes must be checked; unverifiable material gets paraphrased or dropped.

---

## Grace / Undeserved Favor

- **John Newton** and "Amazing Grace" — slaver turned hymn-writer; well-documented (cite his autobiography or standard hymn histories).
- **Horatio Spafford** and "It Is Well with My Soul" — wrote the hymn after his daughters drowned in the Atlantic; well-documented.
- **Charles Spurgeon** sermons frequently illustrate grace; verifiable in his published sermons.
- **Ellen White** on grace: *Steps to Christ*, esp. chapters "The Sinner's Need of Christ," "Repentance," "Confession," "Consecration." Verify quotes via egwwritings.org.
- **Augustine** in *Confessions* — pear-tree theft and reflections on grace.
- **The prodigal son** retold from a fresh angle (Tim Keller's *The Prodigal God* reframes it well; cite Keller).

---

## Sabbath / Rest

- **Joseph Bates and the Sabbath truth** — his journey from sea captain to Sabbath-keeper; well documented in *Life Sketches* / *Origin and Progress of the Seventh-day Adventists*.
- **Eric Liddell** and the 1924 Olympics — refused to run on Sunday; documented in biographies and *Chariots of Fire*.
- **The Jewish tradition of Shabbat** — well-documented practices of stopping, blessing, family meals (Heschel's *The Sabbath* is a rich source; cite him).
- **Ellen White** on Sabbath: *Patriarchs and Prophets* chapters on Creation and Sinai; *Desire of Ages* chapters on Mark 2 / Luke 6 conflicts.

---

## Second Coming / Hope

- **William Miller** and the 1844 Adventist movement — Bible study leading to the Great Disappointment; well documented in Adventist heritage sources.
- **Hiram Edson's cornfield experience** — October 23, 1844 — vision of Christ entering the heavenly sanctuary. Documented in Adventist heritage.
- **Pioneers' deathbed testimonies** — many Adventist pioneers spoke of awaiting the resurrection.
- **C.S. Lewis** in *The Last Battle* — the closing chapters as a vivid picture of new creation; cite the work.

---

## State of the Dead / Resurrection Hope

- **Ellen White** in *Great Controversy*, chapter "Can Our Dead Speak to Us?" — direct treatment.
- **D.L. Moody's deathbed words** — "Earth recedes, heaven opens before me…" — well documented in his biographies.
- **The widow of Nain, Jairus's daughter, Lazarus** retold with the Adventist lens that resurrection is the hope, not disembodied immortality.

---

## The Sanctuary / Christ Our High Priest

- **Hiram Edson's cornfield experience** (see above) — foundational Adventist sanctuary illustration.
- **The Day of Atonement** as practiced in Second Temple Judaism — well-documented background.
- **Court records and advocates** — courtroom scenes are classic for illustrating Christ as advocate. Use real cases (e.g., a famous defense lawyer) where you can verify; otherwise frame as "imagine."
- **Ellen White** in *Patriarchs and Prophets* (sanctuary chapters), *Great Controversy* (sanctuary and judgment chapters).

---

## Faith / Trust in Trial

- **Corrie ten Boom** — *The Hiding Place*; the watch shop, the prison, the smuggled Bible; her testimony of forgiveness toward a former camp guard. Well-documented.
- **Dietrich Bonhoeffer** — *The Cost of Discipleship*; his return to Germany; his execution. Cite Bethge's biography.
- **Daniel** — the lions' den; biblical, but also rich for retelling.
- **Hudson Taylor** in China — many well-documented stories of trust and provision.
- **George Müller** and the orphanages in Bristol — provision stories; documented in his journals.

---

## Service / The Poor / Ministry

- **Mother Teresa** — but verify quotes; many are fabricated. Stick to documented episodes from her work in Calcutta.
- **Adventist medical mission stories** — Loma Linda's history, mission hospitals; documented in *Adventist Heritage*.
- **Wesley's "do all the good you can" formula** — verify exact wording before quoting.
- **Florence Nightingale, William Wilberforce** — well-documented reformers.

---

## Suffering / Lament / Grief

- **Job** — biblical, retold with fresh attention to the actual ash-heap scene.
- **Spafford** (see above).
- **C.S. Lewis** — *A Grief Observed* after his wife's death; cite the work.
- **Ellen White** on suffering — *Education*, chapter "Lessons of Life"; *Ministry of Healing*, chapter "In Contact with Nature."

---

## Forgiveness / Reconciliation

- **Corrie ten Boom** and the Ravensbrück guard.
- **Joseph and his brothers** (Genesis 45, 50) — biblical.
- **Lincoln on his enemies** ("Do I not destroy my enemies when I make them my friends?") — verify; sometimes attributed but contested.
- **Spurgeon** on forgiveness in his published sermons.

---

## Salvation by Faith / Justification

- **Martin Luther** — the Tower experience; the 95 theses; "the just shall live by faith." Well-documented but be careful with the exact wording of the Tower experience.
- **John Wesley's heart "strangely warmed"** — May 24, 1738, Aldersgate; documented in his journal.
- **The 1888 Minneapolis Conference** in Adventist history — Jones, Waggoner, and the rediscovery of righteousness by faith. Documented.
- **Ellen White** on righteousness by faith: *Selected Messages* vol. 1; *Christ's Object Lessons*, chapter on the Pharisee and publican.

---

## Health and the Body

- **Daniel 1** — the test of the diet at Babylon; biblical.
- **Ellen White** in *Ministry of Healing*, *Counsels on Diet and Foods*.
- **John Harvey Kellogg and the Battle Creek Sanitarium** — Adventist health-reform history; verify specifics.
- **Modern research on Blue Zones** — Loma Linda is one of the five Blue Zones identified by Dan Buettner; cite *National Geographic* / Buettner's work.

---

## Cross / Atonement

- **Ellen White** in *Desire of Ages*, chapters on Gethsemane, the trial, the cross. Verify exact quotes (DA p. 25, p. 753, etc.).
- **Spurgeon's** vast body of cross-centered illustrations.
- **Augustus Toplady** and "Rock of Ages" — hymn history.
- **Isaac Watts** and "When I Survey the Wondrous Cross" — hymn history.

---

## End-Time / Three Angels' Messages

- **William Miller and the Millerite movement** — broad Adventist heritage.
- **Ellen White** in *Great Controversy*.
- **Reformation history** — Wycliffe, Hus, Tyndale, Luther; all well-documented.

---

## Indonesian-context illustrations (where applicable)

- **The founding of Adventist work in Indonesia** — Ralph Munson (1900); UNAI history; Klabat history.
- **Indonesian Adventist mission stories** — Sumatra, Sulawesi, Papua frontier mission accounts in *Mission* quarterly archives.
- **Local cultural touchpoints** — Indonesian proverbs, kampung life, gotong-royong, the experience of being a religious minority in some regions.

---

## Sources NOT to use without verification

- **Einstein, Lincoln, Mother Teresa, Gandhi, C.S. Lewis quotes** — internet meme quotes are frequently fabricated. Verify via reputable sources or skip.
- **"Starfish on the beach," "carrot-egg-coffee," "the lighthouse and the ships," "the dash on the tombstone"** — clichéd; if used, only as a foil ("you've heard the starfish story; here's something better").
- **Pop-psychology studies** — "the marshmallow test," "Milgram experiment" — often misremembered; check the actual research.

---

## When in doubt: paraphrase, attribute, and move on

A clean, briefly-told story with honest attribution beats a flashy story with a fabricated quote every time. The pulpit is no place for "close enough."
